AxoZ Data Deletion

AxoZ Data Deletion Request

Effective date: June 27, 2026

AxoZ - Productivity made easier is developed by AxoNodes. This page explains how users can delete local app data or request deletion of data that AxoNodes can reasonably identify.

No account required

AxoZ does not require users to create an account, sign in, or use an external login. Because of this, AxoNodes does not maintain user account profiles for AxoZ.

Local app data

Tasks, notes, habits, and expense records created in AxoZ are stored locally on the user’s device.

How to delete local data

Users can delete local AxoZ data by:

  • Deleting individual tasks, notes, habits, or expenses inside the app.
  • Clearing AxoZ app storage from Android settings.
  • Uninstalling AxoZ from the device.

Request deletion from AxoNodes

If you contacted AxoNodes for support or voluntarily sent app-related information, you can request deletion by emailing:

work@axonodes.fun

Please use the subject line: AxoZ Data Deletion Request

Information to include

  • Your request to delete AxoZ-related data.
  • The email address you used to contact AxoNodes, if different.
  • Any details that help us identify your support request or app-related message.

Data that may be deleted

  • Support emails or messages sent to AxoNodes.
  • App-related records or attachments voluntarily provided to AxoNodes.
  • Other AxoZ-related information that can reasonably be linked to the deletion request.

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or compliance reasons. Backup copies may remain for up to 90 days before deletion.

Third-party services

AxoZ uses Google AdMob and Firebase services. Advertising, analytics, crash diagnostics, and messaging data may be processed by Google according to Google and Firebase policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google My Ad Center
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

Response time

AxoNodes will review deletion requests and respond within a reasonable time. We may request additional information if needed to identify the data related to your request.

Contact

For data deletion or privacy questions, contact AxoNodes at work@axonodes.fun.
