AxoZ Data Deletion Request

App: AxoZ - Productivity made easier

Developer: AxoNodes

No account required

AxoZ does not require users to create an account or sign in. Tasks, notes, habits, and expenses are stored locally on the user’s device.

How to delete local app data

You can delete tasks, notes, habits, and expenses inside the AxoZ app. You can also remove local app data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s storage from Android settings.

Request deletion

To request deletion of support records or data that AxoNodes can reasonably identify, email us at:

work@axonodes.fun

Use the subject line: AxoZ Data Deletion Request

Data that may be deleted

  • Support emails or messages you sent to AxoNodes
  • Any app-related records that can reasonably be linked to your request

Data that may be kept

Some data may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or compliance reasons. Backup copies may remain for up to 90 days before deletion.

Third-party services

AxoZ uses Google AdMob and Firebase services. Some advertising, analytics, crash, diagnostic, and messaging data may be processed by Google according to Google and Firebase policies.
