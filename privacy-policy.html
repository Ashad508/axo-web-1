Privacy Policy for AxoZ

Effective date: June 27, 2026

AxoZ - Productivity made easier is a productivity application developed by AxoNodes. This Privacy Policy explains how AxoZ handles information when you use the app.

Information you create in the app

AxoZ lets you create tasks, notes, habits, and expense records. This content is stored locally on your device. AxoNodes does not require you to create an account, sign in, or upload this content to AxoNodes servers.

Advertising

AxoZ uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. Google AdMob may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interactions, approximate location, diagnostic information, and other data used for advertising, fraud prevention, analytics, and service improvement. AdMob may use this information according to Google’s policies and your consent choices.

Firebase services

AxoZ uses Firebase services provided by Google:

  • Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve app features.
  • Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports, diagnostics, device information, and performance-related data to help fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver app update notices or other important app notifications.

Notifications

AxoZ may request notification permission so it can send task reminders, daily pending-task reminders, and app update notices. You can allow or deny notification permission from your device settings. Notification messages are used for app functionality and productivity reminders.

Data collected by third-party services

Third-party services used by AxoZ may collect data such as device or other identifiers, advertising ID, crash logs, diagnostics, app interactions, and approximate location where applicable. These services process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google AdMob Privacy Information

How information is used

Information may be used to provide app functionality, show advertisements, measure app usage, diagnose crashes, improve reliability, prevent fraud or abuse, and send notifications related to AxoZ.

Data sharing

AxoZ does not sell your personal task, note, habit, or expense content. Data may be processed by Google services used in the app, including AdMob and Firebase, as described above.

Data security

Data transmitted by Google AdMob and Firebase services is encrypted in transit. Local app content remains stored on your device unless you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app.

Data deletion

You can delete tasks, notes, habits, and expenses inside the AxoZ app. You can also clear local data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from Android settings.

To request deletion of support records or data that AxoNodes can reasonably identify, visit:

https://www.axonodes.fun/data-deletion.html

Or email: work@axonodes.fun

Children’s privacy

AxoZ is not directed to children under 13. AxoZ does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

AxoNodes may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
