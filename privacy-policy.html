Privacy Policy for AxoZ

Effective date: June 15, 2026

AxoZ - Productivity made easier stores the tasks, notes, habits, and expenses you enter locally on your Android device. AxoZ does not operate an account system or upload this productivity content to an AxoZ server.

Device storage and backup

Your productivity content remains on your device until you delete entries, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Android backup or device-transfer features may copy local data according to your device and Google account settings.

Advertising and consent

AxoZ uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform to collect or manage privacy choices where required. These services may process IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention according to your consent choices, device settings, applicable law, and Google's policies.

Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. Available advertising privacy choices can be reviewed from the AxoZ settings screen.

Sharing and sale

AxoZ does not sell the tasks, notes, habits, or expense information you enter. Google may process advertising-related information as described above.

Children

AxoZ is intended for a general audience and is not designed primarily for children.

Security

Local content is protected by Android application sandboxing. Advertising-service data is encrypted in transit according to Google's SDK disclosure. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its service providers change. The effective date above identifies this revision.

Contact
