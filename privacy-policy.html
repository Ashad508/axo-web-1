AxoZ Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for AxoZ

Effective date: June 27, 2026

AxoZ - Productivity made easier is a productivity app developed by AxoNodes. This Privacy Policy explains how AxoZ handles information when you use the app.

Information you create

AxoZ lets users create tasks, notes, habits, and expense records. This content is stored locally on the user’s device. AxoZ does not require account creation, sign in, or external login.

Advertising

AxoZ uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob may collect or process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interactions, approximate location, diagnostics, and related data for advertising, fraud prevention, analytics, and service improvement.

Firebase services

AxoZ uses Firebase services provided by Google:

  • Firebase Analytics for app usage analytics and feature improvement.
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports, diagnostics, and stability improvement.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging for app update notices and important app notifications.

Notifications

AxoZ may request notification permission to send task reminders, daily pending-task reminders, and app update notices. Users can allow, deny, or disable notifications from device settings.

Data collected by third-party services

Third-party services used by AxoZ may collect data such as device identifiers, advertising ID, crash logs, diagnostics, app interactions, and approximate location where applicable.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google AdMob Privacy Information

How information is used

Information may be used to provide app functionality, show ads, measure app usage, diagnose crashes, improve reliability, prevent fraud or abuse, and send app-related notifications.

Data sharing

AxoZ does not sell user-created tasks, notes, habits, or expense content. Data may be processed by Google services used in the app, including AdMob and Firebase.

Data security

Data transmitted by Google AdMob and Firebase services is encrypted in transit. Local app content remains stored on the user’s device unless deleted, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Data deletion

Users can delete tasks, notes, habits, and expenses inside AxoZ. Users can also clear local data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from Android settings.

Data deletion requests can be made here: https://www.axonodes.fun/data-deletion.html

Children’s privacy

AxoZ is not directed to children under 13. AxoZ does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

For questions, contact AxoNodes at work@axonodes.fun.
